Our process

A clear sequence, so you always know what happens next.

Consultation

We discuss your goals, site, timeline and budget, and agree the scope of work.

Concept design

Sketches, layouts and mood references that explore the best ways to use the site.

Design development

We refine the chosen concept, resolve materials and finishes, and prepare approvals.

Construction drawings

Detailed technical drawings and specifications for pricing and building.

On-site supervision

Regular visits and coordination with contractors so quality and budget stay on track.

Handover

A final walkthrough, snag list and documents, so you move into a finished space.
